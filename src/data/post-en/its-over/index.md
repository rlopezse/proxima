for several weeks now i've been watching youtube videos, scrolling through twitter and other social media, and i think that at this point ai is inevitable in the world of software development, and there's no turning back.

i just watched a video by @dhh in which he expresses perfectly how i feel right now. writing code by hand, until not long ago, was the norm, an art, something you could spend hours and hours perfecting, polishing every detail. i still remember the joke: i spent hours trying to fix this bug and the problem was a missing ;, something that today, with all the technological progress we have, sounds bad in taste to hear someone say.

in the video he says he used to spend hours writing code, that finding a solution gave him a great sense of satisfaction, but that moment came to an end, and now basically what used to be done by hand turned into giving instructions to ai so it does it for you.

as i listened to the video it was as if a farmer said: i spent decades farming my land by hand, i suffered under the sun, but now that i have a tractor i no longer need to do it by hand, and that's fine, it's a change that both i and everyone else should accept as part of evolution. tractors revolutionized agriculture just like ai is doing with software. you no longer need to till the soil with your hands, just like you don't need to write all the code by hand.

### nostalgia for the past and hope for the future

i went back to using claude on my projects, now not just as a "fix this specific error" but at 100%: i give instructions, they get executed, i review, validate and accept the change, kind of like being a project manager (the difference is that here the ai doesn't get tired and does everything in record time). it's pretty scary to think about what it could end up doing, but it also unlocks an endless flow of ideas and saves a huge amount of time that's now free for other things and for learning new ones.

### is it over?

i'd say no, tractors didn't take jobs away from farmers, they simply made them more efficient, and ai doesn't take jobs away from developers, it simply makes them more efficient, and lets us do things that used to be impossible because of a lack of time or resources.

the advantage of ai is that it can explain to you why it made a change, like a teacher would, and give you all the resources you need to learn and improve. maybe the way of writing code by hand is over, but the way of learning, improving and delivering solutions is more alive than ever, and i like that: one door closed, for better or worse, but many others opened, giving us endless possibilities.
